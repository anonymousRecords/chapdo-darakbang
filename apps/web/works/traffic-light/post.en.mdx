---
title: "Traffic Lights: Rediscovering an Everyday Sight"
---

One day, someone I knew asked me, “Can traffic lights break down too? What happens when they do?”

When building services, I often imagine things going wrong—and sometimes encounter those failures firsthand. But what about the traffic lights we see every week? Surely they must fail occasionally too. Why had I never noticed? I found myself thinking about those questions.

From that day on, I began paying closer attention to traffic lights. Traveling abroad also inspired me with the different designs used in other countries. Bringing those thoughts together, I observed the traffic lights here in Korea and recreated them myself.
