---
title: "The Audio Fader in the School Broadcast Room: What Tactile Sensation Do You Remember?"
---

I was part of the broadcasting club in elementary school.

I joined simply because it seemed cool. Of the various roles, including announcer and camera operator, I chose the one that appealed to me most: producer.

My main job was to play videos during morning assemblies and school events, and to adjust the volume using an audio fader.

Even now, I sometimes remember how that fader felt. Its slightly concave surface fit my finger just right. Instead of sliding perfectly smoothly, it moved with a faint series of clicks—a subtle resistance I could feel under my fingertip.

I tried to recreate that tactile memory that still comes back to me from time to time.
